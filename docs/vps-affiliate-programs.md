## Detailed Notes by Provider

### 1. ihor.online
- Simple cookie-based referral link + banner system
- Earnings usable for own services or bank withdrawal (contract required for bank)
- No tiered structure — flat 5% regardless of volume

### 2. ruvds.com
- Best for high-volume referrers who can grow into 20-25% tiers
- Admitad network integration available (separate 36% first-month CPA deal)
- 10% withdrawal tax reduces effective rate (15% → ~13.5%)

### 3. the.hosting
- Strongest tier structure among small providers — reaches 20% at 100 active referrals
- Recurring lifetime commissions are the key value proposition
- Banners and individual promo materials for large partners

### 4. ishosting.com
- Highest raw commission rate on VPS among cookie-based programs (up to 20%)
- Crypto withdrawal is a differentiator for international affiliates
- Lifetime commissions grow with number of referred clients

### 5. timeweb.cloud
- 40% on classic hosting is industry-leading for that category
- Very low minimum payout (390₽) — easiest to cash out
- Separate webmaster portal (wm.timeweb.ru) vs. cloud partner program

### 6. robovps.biz
- 120-day cookie is among the longest for RU providers
- 20% on virtual hosting is strong; 5% on VPS is weak
- 3,000₽ minimum is accessible

### 7. serverspace.io
- International platform (available in USD/EUR)
- Time-decaying model (10% year 1, 5% after) rewards quick conversions
- Calculator widget embeddable on any site — unusual tool

### 8. adminvps.ru
- 40% on standard hosting; flat 500₽ bonus per dedicated server
- Promo code system allows partner to give client discount while still earning commission
- Also available via Admitad CPA network

### 9. ufo.hosting
- Lowest commission rate (3%) of all providers with programs
- Simple in-dashboard access, no external portal

### 10. omro.host
- Low minimum payout (€10) — accessible for small affiliates
- Good rate on hosting (40%), weaker on VPS (15%)
- Newer provider (est. 2022) — smaller referral pool

### 11. selectel.ru
- Enterprise-grade program with highest volume potential
- No cookie expiry — referral link binding is permanent
- 10,000₽ minimum payout is high but payout cap (1M₽/month) is generous
- Referrals spending >1M₽/month earn you 15% — suitable for B2B referrals

### 12. en.prohost.kg
- No affiliate program found. Kyrgyz provider, limited web presence

### 13. vdsina.com
- Best tiered structure for high-revenue affiliates (up to 50%)
- USDT payouts available — unique in this list
- 30-day cookie. Weekly payouts are fast
- 10% alternative (with 10% client discount) is client-acquisition-friendly

### 14. firstbyte.ru
- Conservative 7% flat rate, monthly payout cycle
- Individuals only — no legal entity participation
- Promo code works offline (printed materials, personal conversations)

### 15. fst.kz
- Kazakhstani provider. Footer link to partner program exists but terms not indexed
- Recommend direct outreach to confirm existence and terms

### 16. hostkey.com
- Only provider with multi-level (3-tier) sub-affiliate commissions
- 180-day cookie is longest in the entire list
- EUR-denominated — international program
- €100 minimum + 3 conversions required is a higher barrier
- €20 signup bonus is unique

### 17. fornex.com
- Best for volume affiliates — 50% VPN and 40% hosting at 10+ active orders
- 60-day cookie. Lifetime commissions
- Clean tiered structure shown live in dashboard
- Crypto payouts available

### 18. 4dedic.io
- No affiliate program found on website or in search results
- Contact support for current status

### 19. 4vps.su
- Up to 15% is solid; referral bonus (20% to new client) aids conversion
- No detailed payout terms publicly available

### 20. yandex.cloud
- Corporate/B2B program with 2,500₽/month per client hard cap
- Multiple participation types (agent, reseller, ISV, integrator)
- Suitable for enterprise client introductions, not mass-market affiliate

### 21. cloud.vk.com (VK Cloud)
- B2B/enterprise program — agent model pays % of consumed resources
- Up to 35% commission, negotiated individually
- Contact partner@mcs.mail.ru for terms — no public rate card

### Programs to Prioritize (Recommendations)

**Tier 1 — Best overall for affiliate revenue:**

1. **fornex.com** — Lifetime 50% on VPN / 40% on hosting at volume, 60-day cookie, transparent tier dashboard. Best pure affiliate program in the list.
2. **vdsina.com** — 50% at high volume, weekly payouts, USDT available, 30-day cookie. Best for high-converting traffic.
3. **timeweb.cloud** — 40% on classic hosting with only 390₽ minimum payout. Easiest to monetize at any scale. Huge brand recognition in RuNet.

**Tier 2 — Strong recurring programs:**

4. **ishosting.com** — Lifetime commissions, crypto payout, competitive 20% on VPS. Good for international audiences.
5. **adminvps.ru** — 40% + bonus structure, promo code system, Admitad integration.
6. **selectel.ru** — No cookie expiry (permanent referral binding), 10-15%, enterprise B2B potential. Best for influencers with business-owner audiences.

**Tier 3 — Solid secondary programs:**

7. **hostkey.com** — Only multi-level program (3 tiers deep), 180-day cookie (longest in list), EUR payments. Good for international or build-a-sub-affiliate strategy.
8. **ruvds.com** — Well-known brand, 15-25%, monthly payouts. Good for RU developer audience.
9. **the.hosting** — Lifetime recurring, scales to 20% at 100+ refs. Good for content creators with sustained traffic.

**Skip for now:**
- ufo.hosting (3% — too low)
- firstbyte.ru (7% — low, individuals only)
- ihor.online (5% flat — minimal upside)
- 4vps.su (limited transparency)
- yandex.cloud / cloud.vk.com (B2B enterprise, not suitable for affiliate channel)

### API-Based Referral Tracking

None of the 21 providers publicly document an API for referral tracking. All programs use:
- **Cookie-based tracking** (standard browser cookies with stated durations)
- **Promo code tracking** (adminvps.ru, firstbyte.ru, ruvds.com — for offline use)
- **Account-based registration** (referral ID entered at signup, e.g. robovps.biz)

Providers with **sub-ID / postback support** (useful for programmatic tracking) that may exist but are undocumented:
- timeweb.cloud (uses Admitad network — Admitad supports postback/S2S)
- adminvps.ru (uses Admitad network — same)
- ruvds.com (uses Admitad network — same)

For full API-based tracking (server-to-server postbacks), using these three via **Admitad** is the recommended approach, as Admitad supports S2S postback URLs, sub-IDs, and programmatic reporting APIs.
